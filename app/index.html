Honest note: GTA 6 has no mission replay — a trophy tied to a single story moment cannot be earned again once that moment has passed. Only one trophy is flagged missable: Positive Mental Attitude, the Chapter 1 balcony meditation at Jason's house after "Down to Earth" — do it while you are there and keep a Chapter 1 save. The 12 story trophies and the 5 stranger-final trophies are earned at their story moments and are not missable.
